20 side by side guides to the things donors actually buy, and the things that make no difference at all

Before you buy anything

Nothing on this page changes whether you can donate. Every visit includes the screening the FDA requires for source plasma donation, and the centre staff decide on the day. These comparisons are about comfort, convenience and cost, and several of them conclude that the cheaper option or no purchase at all is the right answer.
