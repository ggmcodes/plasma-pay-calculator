How these reviews are written

Every review here draws on published manufacturer specifications and the consensus of verified owner reviews. We have not tested any of these products in person and we never claim to. Every specification quoted is attributed to its source, every Amazon listing was confirmed live before publishing, and every page has a section naming the product's real weakness, because that is the part worth reading.

Prices move constantly, so treat every figure as approximate and confirm before ordering. Questions about supplements, iron, blood pressure, diet or your fitness to donate belong with the staff at your donation centre and with your own doctor. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.
